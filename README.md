## ✨ Overview

AICE (AI Innovation Community for Excellence) is the AI community of **College of Engineering Chengannur**.

This website presents AICE through a modern, immersive interface focused on:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Innovation
- Learning
- Research
- Collaboration
- Entrepreneurship

The goal was to create a website that feels **interactive, modern, and technology-focused** while keeping the content clear and accessible.

---

## 🎯 What the Website Includes

### Hero Section

Introduces AICE with a strong visual identity and an interactive animated background.

### About AICE

Provides a concise introduction to AICE and its role as an AI community at College of Engineering Chengannur.

### Mission

Highlights AICE's focus on:

> Learn. Experiment. Build.

The section communicates the community's emphasis on AI learning, innovation, research, entrepreneurship, hands-on experiences, and collaboration.

### Vision

Presents AICE's vision of creating a:

> Future-ready AI ecosystem.

### Events

Showcases upcoming AICE activities and events using an interactive event presentation.

### Membership / Join

Encourages students to become part of the AICE community through a dedicated join section.

### Footer

Provides navigation, official links, social media links, and AICE branding.

---

## 🎨 Design

The website follows a dark, minimal, futuristic visual language inspired by modern AI and technology interfaces.

### Design principles

- Dark-first interface
- High-contrast typography
- Purple accent system
- Large editorial typography
- Glass-like cards
- Interactive visual effects
- Smooth scrolling
- Responsive layouts
- Motion-driven section transitions

The animations are designed to enhance the experience without taking attention away from the content.

---

## ⚡ Interactive Experiences

The website uses several interactive visual components throughout the page.

### Hero

Interactive particle-based visual background.

### About

Animated particle field responding to user interaction.

### Mission

Interactive dot-field visualization with cursor interaction.

### Vision

Procedural WebGL orb visualization.

### Events Transition

Animated strand-based visual transition between sections.

### Scroll Experience

Smooth scrolling and stacked card interactions create a continuous storytelling experience between the About, Mission, and Vision sections.
